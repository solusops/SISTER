Model-run notes
===============

Retained completed models
-------------------------

GPT-OSS 20B
- Fastest model in this set.
- Reasoning could not be reliably disabled: LM Studio exposed low, medium, and
  high rather than off. Treat all collected GPT-OSS outputs as reasoning-enabled.

Gemma 4 12B QAT
- Thinking controls were inconsistent or ignored through the UI/API.
- The successful run used no explicit thinking field and a zero thinking budget.

Llama 3.1 8B
- No result-data failure.
- Sustained inference required monitoring GPU thermals; the final 16K run is clean.

Mistral 7B Instruct v0.3
- The earlier target/version and 32K KV-cache planning caused confusion.
- The final v0.3 Q4_K_M run used 16K and completed cleanly.

Granite 4 H Tiny
- Fifteen 16K context-length stops contaminated sharded trajectories.
- Removed 87 rows across 14 affected trajectories before continuation.
- A later overflow retried with seed 12346; one abandoned normal-stop orphan
  row was also removed.
- Final active result has 1,156 clean rows.

Qwen 3.5 9B
- Default behavior attempted hidden thinking despite normal controls.
- A custom no-thinking template plus zero thinking budget produced normal output.
- Two 16K context-length stops required controlled continuation.
- The final segment used a 22,528-token context window and completed cleanly.
